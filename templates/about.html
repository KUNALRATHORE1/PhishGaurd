About PhishGuard

PhishGuard is an educational, rule-based phishing risk assessment tool built with Python and Flask. It helps users get a quick sense of how "phishing-like" a message, screenshot, or website link appears — based entirely on transparent, explainable heuristics rather than AI or machine learning.

How the Danger Score Works

Each analysis scans the content against a configurable dictionary of phishing indicators (urgent language, OTP/password requests, banking keywords, prize scams, suspicious URLs, and more). Every matched category adds weighted points to a Danger Score between 0 and 100. The score is then mapped to a risk level:

  • 0–25: Safe
  • 26–50: Low Risk
  • 51–75: Medium Risk
  • 76–100: High Risk

What PhishGuard Does NOT Do

  • It does not use AI, machine learning, or any paid API.
  • It does not visit or fetch the content of analyzed URLs — only the URL's structure/text is examined.
  • It does not claim definitive phishing detection — results are a risk estimate only.
  • It does not store, share, or transmit your data anywhere — analysis happens locally.

Technology Stack

  • Python + Flask (backend and routing)
  • EasyOCR (screenshot text extraction)
  • Pillow (image validation)
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript (frontend)

Disclaimer: PhishGuard is intended for educational purposes only. It is a student/portfolio project and should not be relied upon as a sole method of phishing detection. Always exercise your own judgement and verify suspicious communications through official channels.
